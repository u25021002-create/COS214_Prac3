Detailed Description

Composite with its own policy: an area whose exhibits stand outdoors.

DisplayZone is an EventGroup that reacts to a weather alert from its own state rather than from the types of its children: an outdoor display area decides that everything it is responsible for must be brought under cover, and says so by broadcasting to its own observers.
